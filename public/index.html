What we do

Three crafts, one studio.

Vector Artwork

Clean, scalable vector illustration built for reproduction — as comfortable on a hang tag as it is on a wall mural.

  • Custom character & mascot art
  • Logo redraws & vector tracing
  • Line art prepped for stitching or print

Embroidery Digitizing

Artwork converted into clean, efficient stitch files — correct density, underlay, and sequencing for your machine.

  • Logo & left-chest digitizing
  • Large-format & jacket-back files
  • DST, PES, EMB and all major formats

Virtual Mockups

Photorealistic previews of artwork on apparel and products, so you can see the final result before it's ever stitched or printed.

  • Apparel & product mockups
  • Multiple colorways & placements
  • Client-ready presentation files

"Every stitch has to earn its place — same as every line in a drawing."

Bluechip Creations was established in 2014 as a studio built around one idea: artwork should hold up no matter what it's made into — a vector file, a stitched patch, or a mockup on screen.

Backed by 29 years of graphic design experience, we work across three disciplines that naturally overlap. Vector artwork gives brands clean, scalable illustration. Embroidery digitizing turns that artwork into files that stitch cleanly on the first run. Virtual mockups let clients see it all on a real product before a single stitch is sewn or a file is printed.

We work with apparel brands, embroidery shops, and small businesses who need artwork that translates cleanly from screen to fabric to finished product.
